Support — Khao

Khao is the app that decides what you eat tonight — and learns your taste with every swipe. This page is the support contact for the iOS app.

Contact

Email anirudhkapoor1996@gmail.com with any question, bug, or idea. You'll normally hear back within 48 hours.

Common questions

How do I reset what Khao has learned?
Profile (More → You) → Delete all my data. This clears your palate, history and settings on the device.

How do I change language?
More → You → Settings → Language. English and Hindi ship today; regional languages are on the way.

How do I report a community post?
Long-press any post → Report. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Where is my data stored?
On your device. Khao has no account system and no analytics — see the privacy policy.
