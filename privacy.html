Privacy Policy — Khao

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Khao is designed to be private. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does and does not do with information. It is deliberately short because Khao collects very little.

The short version

Khao has no account system and no analytics. Everything you do in the app — your taste preferences, diet, meal times, likes, saved dishes, and anything you post — is stored only on your device. Khao does not operate a server that receives your personal data.

What stays on your device

The following is saved locally on your iPhone (in the app's own storage) and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your language, diet, allergen and dislike settings
  • The dishes you like, skip, or mark "never again" (how Khao learns your palate)
  • Your meal times and reminder preferences
  • Recipes and meal offers you create in Community and Khao Share

You can erase all of it at any time from Profile → Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Khao schedules local meal reminders on your device. These are generated on the phone; no reminder content is sent through us. The app also registers with Apple Push Notification service, but Khao does not transmit your device token to any Khao server (no such server is configured), so no push data leaves your device today.

Images

A small number of dish photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons (a public, freely-licensed image source) when shown. Loading an image exposes your device's IP address to Wikimedia, as with any image on the web. Khao sends no personal information with these requests. Photo attributions are listed in the app under Profile → Photo credits.

Ordering out

When you tap to order a dish, Khao opens Zomato or Swiggy in your browser or their app. Anything you do there is governed by their privacy policies, not this one.

Community content

Recipes and meal offers you post are visible to other Khao users. Please don't include personal information you don't want shared. You can delete your own posts, and report or block others, from within the app. We remove content that violates our community rules, reviewed within 24 hours.

Children

Khao is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Data we sell or share

None. Khao does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. Khao shows no ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be noted in the app.
